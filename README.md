1  # MovieFinder
2
3  A personal, non-commercial Windows desktop application for looking up movie and TV
4  metadata with the TMDB API.
5
6  It searches movies and TV shows by title (Chinese, English, or aliases) and shows the
7  original title, release year, poster, rating, genres, runtime and overview.
8
9  It does not host, stream, download or distribute any video content, does not redistribute
10 TMDB data, has no advertising and no commercial use. It runs locally on my own computer.
